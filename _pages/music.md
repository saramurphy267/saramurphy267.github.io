In my free time I like to play bluegrass and old-time music. These days, I've been playing music for contra and square dances as part of a three-piece band called the Live Oak String Band. Recently, I also played on the [Wildwood Flower Radio Hour](https://www.wildwoodflowerradiohour.com/) at LMU with other Los Angeles-based bluegrass musicians, calling ourselves the 4th of July Bluegrass Band (we formed our pickup band at a 4th of July barbeque). You can listen to those sessions [here](https://soundcloud.com/wildwood-flower-radio/wwfrh-show-125-with-devon) and [here](https://soundcloud.com/wildwood-flower-radio/show-100-1724-with-jems-the-4th-of-july-bluegrass-band-guest-host-david-elsenbroich)! 

I have also played music with my family throughout my life and have recorded on two albums, one with the Murphy Family Band (*My Window Faces the South*) and one as the fiddle player on my brother Kyle Murphy's album (*Eucalyptus*). If you're interested, some tracks from these albums are linked below: 

**[1. December Waltz/Richland](https://youtu.be/yFRL1levlZQ?si=cTWgkIospfRkRydu)**. A duet on *Eucalyptus* with me playing fiddle and my brother playing mandolin. I wrote the first tune (December Waltz), and he wrote the second (Richland).

**[2. Morning Waltz](https://youtu.be/R1jUaZCHWRg?si=uUTaBlUNKaQpWLbD)**. A waltz I wrote on *Eucalyptus*, with my dad Jim Murphy on guitar and Joshua Riffe playing the bass. 

**[3. Dusty Miller](https://youtu.be/Wgp5V-35ZjY?si=Qpe1hXvJk6tZugwF)**. A classic bluegrass fiddle tune that we recorded for *My Window Faces the South*.  

**[4. You Low Down Dirty Dog](https://youtu.be/8nC8g3wsVvs?si=QQCyvrsgVIMYzcqR)**. A fun old-timey style song that has lyrics designed entirely to find words that rhyme with dog. 

I plan to continue playing bluegrass and old-time music and hope to contribute to the music community wherever I end up!
