As the instructor of record for the General Chemistry Laboratory (CHEM 120L) at Occidental College, I had the opportunity to run laboratory experiments, explain fundamental concepts of chemistry, and assist students with learning laboratory skills.

Having the opportunity to teach primarily first-year students in this course helped me develop my skills in teaching fundamental and introdcutory chemistry concepts. Supporting students in what for many was their first laboratory experience was extremely rewarding, and I look forward to teaching more laboratory courses in the future. Below are some quotes from student evaluations from this course.

"In high school I did not do any official labs so this was really eye opening and I am glad that I had a good professor who was able to guide me through the whole process when I had questions."

"I loved this lab. The professor made sure that everyone knew what they were doing and was super approachable"

"Professor Murphy did well in not only creating a nice lab environment, but also chatting with the students and asking about our days promoted our success as a whole because we felt safer and more comfortable" 

"Really loved lab, the instructor was super kind and helpful with instruction and had a good balance of helping us with what we needed to be doing but also leaving us enough room for ourselves to figure things out on our own. The professor also had lots of fun stories about their experience in chemistry that I enjoyed hearing and was veery sociable!"
