As the instructor of record for Physical Chemistry II at Occidental College, I wrote and presented lectures on Quantum Chemistry, Spectroscopy, and Group Theory, wrote and graded problem sets and exams, and provided support to students.

Teaching this course cemented my desire to teach undergraduates and helped me develop my teaching philosophy and style. I learned how best to facilitate group work, how to adapt future lectures for effectiveness, and how to support students with detailed feedback and office hours. Below are quotes from student evaluations after the culmination of the course and a PDF of the course syllabus.
